Error Handling Tips ​

💡 Tip:

  • When encountering errors, check the returned trace_id, which helps us troubleshoot
  • For 4xx errors (client errors), check your request parameters and authentication information
  • For 5xx errors (server errors), it's recommended to retry later. If the issue persists, contact the support team
  • 429 errors can usually be resolved by reducing request frequency or upgrading your plan

Need Help? ​

If you encounter errors not listed here, or need further assistance, please check our Quickstart Guide or contact the support team.
